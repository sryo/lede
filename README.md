# Lede

Your site's stats, told as a story.

<img src="lede.png" width="400" alt="Lede's home screen: 67,208 visitors, up 6%. Google brought 2,092 of the 3,507 extra visitors, mostly to /migration.">

It runs free on Cloudflare for most sites, uses no cookies (so no cookie banner), and your stats stay yours.

## Install

[![Deploy to Cloudflare](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/button)](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/?url=https://github.com/sryo/lede)

1. Pick a password for your dashboard.
2. Make a key so Lede can read your stats. The form links to it, ready to go.
3. Open your dashboard, type your site's address, and paste the line it gives you into your site.

[How it works](docs/how-it-works.md)
